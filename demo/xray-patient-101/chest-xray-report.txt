EXAMINATION
-----------
Chest radiograph, frontal view (Panel A).
CT chest, axial section, lung window (Panel B).

CLINICAL NOTE
-------------
3-month-old infant presenting with respiratory distress.

FINDINGS
--------
Chest radiograph shows a cystic hyperlucent left lung field with visible
septations (arrow) and mass effect with mediastinal shift. Appearances were
initially mistaken for a pneumatocele and a chest tube was inserted.

CT chest axial section with lung window shows a thin-walled cyst in the left
lower lobe with mass effect. No pneumothorax. No pleural effusion.

IMPRESSION
----------
Congenital cystic adenomatoid malformation (CCAM), type 1, left lower lobe.
Diagnosis confirmed after surgery on histopathology in the source study.

TEACHING NOTE (from the source study)
-------------------------------------
CCAM involving the lower lobes can resemble a pneumothorax by showing lucency
across the lung field; the presence of septations within the cystic lucent
lesion, visible vascular markings, and relative sparing of the apical region
point toward CCAM. CT was diagnostic in all study cases and helped localise
the lesion and detect chest-tube position.
